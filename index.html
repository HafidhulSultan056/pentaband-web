01 — Hardware

Arsitektur Perangkat

LoRa Receiver

Sub-GHz wireless module for cellular-independent disaster alerts (up to 5 km range).

Dual Vibration Motors

Left and right micro-motors for haptic directional cues.

Passive NFC / Engraved QR

Contactless medical data retrieval requiring zero internal power.

BLE Module & Battery

Bluetooth Low Energy for safe-zone detection powered by a 30-day standby Li-Po cell.
